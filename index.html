About

I started out in chemical engineering, graduating cum laude from Mindanao State University - Iligan Institute of Technology in 2023 and becoming a licensed chemical engineer in 2024. My thesis on polymer synthesis optimization was awarded College Best Thesis in Sustainability.

After graduating, I worked as a cadet engineer at Coco Corporation, where I monitored biodiesel production through a Distributed Control System. Working with real-time plant data got me interested in what could be done with it, which led me to machine learning.

I am now taking a Master of Engineering in Artificial Intelligence at the University of the Philippines Diliman. My work so far focuses on classical machine learning, feature engineering, and building models that are lightweight and interpretable.

Projects

Philippine Medicinal Plant Leaf Classification

Python | OpenCV | scikit-learn | UMAP

A classical machine learning pipeline that classifies 4,922 leaf images across 40 Philippine medicinal plant species. I extracted a 371-feature vector per image (color, texture, shape, and vein descriptors), selected the top 200 features using ANOVA F-scores, and tuned an RBF SVM with grid search.

Result: 98.06% test accuracy, beating the published VGG19 CNN benchmark of 92.67% and landing within 0.33 points of a VGG19 + SVM baseline, with a much lighter model.
